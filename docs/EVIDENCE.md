### Proving Time Note (Row E-02)
User-side proving time on CPU (`145.69 s` for `ClaimExact`, `166.74 s` for one-time `token::InitializeHolding`) is overwhelmingly dominated by LEZ's built-in privacy circuit (two ML-KEM-768 encapsulations and shielded-state padding to 7 actions — roughly 85–96% of total proving time). Escapement's public execution adds only `58,375` cycles (`0.58%` of cap). Two practical consequences are confirmed directly by this measurement:
1. Using `Response::timestamp_window(effective_ts..)` instead of `clock_actor()` is mandatory so a 2–5 minute CPU proof never expires before inclusion.
2. Designing `escapement_core_module` with persistent async proving jobs is mandatory because `145.69 s` exceeds Logos Basecamp's 20-second `callModuleAsync` timeout by 7×.
